Scope discipline

When a custom quote is mandatory.

  • More than 2,500 voice minutes per month
  • More than two locations, numbers, integrations, or an additional language
  • Private APIs, write access to a system of record, or custom application UI
  • Outbound calling beyond a narrow warm-lead or customer workflow
  • Payments, identity verification, healthcare, legal, financial, minors', or other sensitive data
  • 24/7 support, contractual uptime, SSO, DPA/BAA, data residency, or enterprise procurement
  • Compressed timelines, third-party minimums, or unusually complex approval structures
No silent scope expansion. If technical feasibility is uncertain, RouteCraft may recommend paid discovery before quoting implementation. If budget is constrained, scope is reduced rather than hidden.

Frequently asked

Before you buy.

Can it book appointments?
A calendar connection may be configured when appropriate. Availability, write behavior, confirmation rules, and platform compatibility are tested before launch. If reliable write access is unavailable, the system can request a preferred slot for staff confirmation.
Which integrations do you support?
Standard calendars, CRMs, field-service tools, email, SMS, and webhooks may be supported. Compatibility is reviewed before it is sold. An unsupported or private system requires discovery and a custom quote.
Is the pilot free?
No. It costs $750 and includes the defined 14-day scope. It is paid validation, not an open-ended free build.
Do you guarantee uptime or revenue?
No. RouteCraft does not guarantee uptime, bookings, revenue, search rankings, call quality, conversion lift, or caller satisfaction unless a signed agreement expressly states a specific service level.
What happens if callers want a human?
The approved fallback can transfer, collect a callback request, route to voicemail, or send a structured notification. Human staffing and response times remain the client's responsibility unless expressly included.
What are the payment terms?
Standard production setup fees are 60% at signature and 40% before production launch. Recurring fees are prepaid monthly. Pilot payment is due in full in advance. A quote is valid for 14 calendar days.
