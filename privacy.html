Privacy policy.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Plain-language summary: We collect the information you choose to submit so we can respond to business inquiries. Simple Analytics records privacy-friendly page and event data without cookies. Do not submit sensitive information through this website.

1. Who we are

RouteCraft Systems is a managed AI front desk and web development business operated by Praneeth Pogula, based in India and serving clients internationally. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to praneethpogula09@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you contact us, we may collect your name, business name, work email, phone number, business type, locations, approximate call volume, current website, systems used, service interest, message, preferred contact method, and consent record.

Website usage data

We use Simple Analytics to collect privacy-friendly page views and named conversion events such as CTA clicks and form-submission attempts. The deployed configuration does not use cookies, fingerprint visitors, create cross-site profiles, or intentionally send form-field values to analytics. Simple Analytics may process URL, referrer, viewport, user agent, country derived from an IP address, timestamp, and event names as described in its own documentation.

3. How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries and evaluate project fit
  • Prepare proposals or arrange a discovery conversation
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve this website
  • Measure aggregate site activity and conversion events
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

Submitting an inquiry does not subscribe you to bulk marketing. Separate consent may be requested for marketing, SMS, or automated calls where required.

4. Service providers and international processing

This site is hosted through GitHub Pages, a Microsoft-owned service. Inquiry forms are processed by FormSubmit and delivered through email providers. Website analytics are processed by Simple Analytics. These providers may process data in countries different from yours under their own privacy terms. Future client systems may also use telephony, speech, AI, messaging, calendar, CRM, hosting, and integration providers disclosed in the relevant agreement.

5. Retention

Inquiry records are retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a possible business relationship, resolve disputes, meet accounting or legal obligations, and maintain suppression or consent records where applicable. We may delete clearly irrelevant or abandoned inquiries. Client-project retention is governed by the applicable agreement and workflow.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a small service business, but no internet transmission, email system, or third-party platform is completely secure. Do not send passwords, payment-card data, government identifiers, medical information, or other sensitive personal data through the website form.

7. AI calls, recordings, and client data

RouteCraft designs systems for clients that may use AI-generated speech and, where enabled, recording or transcription. Clients control the purpose of their call workflows and remain responsible for approved disclosures, consent, lawful use, and accurate business content. RouteCraft may act as a service provider or processor for client-controlled information. People seeking rights regarding a client's call may need to contact that client directly.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or information about processing. These rights may be subject to identity verification and legal exceptions. Send requests to praneethpogula09@gmail.com. If we hold data only for a client, we may direct the request to that client.

9. Children

This website and our business services are intended for organizations and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this site.

10. External links and policy updates

External sites have their own privacy practices. We may update this policy as services, vendors, or legal obligations change. The revised version will be posted here with a new effective date.
