What the foundation includes

Launch essentials, managed together.

The standard scope is intentionally bounded so the site can move from intake to production in roughly 3–4 weeks after content and access are complete.

Conversion path

Up to seven responsive pages organized around one primary action: call, book, or submit a qualified inquiry.

Technical foundation

Titles, descriptions, headings, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots file, SSL, analytics, and agreed conversion events.

Reliable operations

Spam-protected form, tested delivery, hosting, monitoring, backups, CMS/security updates, and two small monthly content requests.

Typical structure

Every page has a job.

  1. 01

    Home

    Position the business, identify the customer problem, and make the primary next step obvious.

  2. 02

    Services

    Clarify scope, fit, exclusions, and the right path for each service type.

  3. 03

    Proof

    Use verifiable work, process, credentials, or customer-approved evidence—never fabricated logos or testimonials.

  4. 04

    About

    Show who is accountable, how the company works, and what it will not promise.

  5. 05

    Contact

    Collect enough information to qualify the inquiry and set an honest response expectation.
