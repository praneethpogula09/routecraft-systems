Delivery method

Control the workflow before live traffic.

We do not throw a prompt at your phone line. The launch process makes responsibilities, boundaries, tests, and fallbacks explicit.

  1. 01

    Map

    Document call types, required fields, approved answers, routing, exceptions, and the human path.

  2. 02

    Build

    Configure the voice, number, knowledge, calendar, and supported integration or handoff.

  3. 03

    Test

    Run normal, edge, prohibited, and failure scenarios. Fix issues before your callers meet the system.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Begin with overflow or after-hours traffic. Expand only after client acceptance and observed behavior.

  5. 05

    Improve

    Review exceptions and approved outcomes monthly instead of treating total call count as success.

Common questions

What responsible automation sounds like.

See all FAQs
Will callers know they are speaking with AI?
Yes. RouteCraft workflows are designed to identify the assistant as AI at or near the start of the interaction.
Can callers reach a person?
The workflow can recognize a request for human help and use the approved fallback. Actual transfer availability and response times depend on the client's staffing and configuration.
Does the agent diagnose HVAC, plumbing, or electrical problems?
No. It is designed for administrative intake, not technical diagnosis, licensed advice, or emergency judgment.
Do you guarantee more bookings?
No. We guarantee the agreed implementation work and report what the system actually did. Demand, availability, offer quality, caller intent, and team follow-up all affect business outcomes.
