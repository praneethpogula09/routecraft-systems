About RouteCraft

Small scope. Clear rules. Managed implementation.

RouteCraft Systems is a managed AI front desk and web development company founded by Praneeth Pogula. Based in India, we serve international service businesses with a launch focus on independent US HVAC, plumbing, and electrical operators.

Why this wedge

The first customer interaction is an operational system.

A phone call is often attached to an urgent service request. Yet the person best equipped to answer may be dispatching, serving a customer, driving, or off the clock.

RouteCraft starts with overflow and after-hours intake because it is concrete, testable, and bounded. We map what the caller needs, what the business may safely say, which fields matter, and when a person must take over.

The goal is not to replace trade expertise or promise automatic growth. It is to create a more organized front door for callers and staff.

Operating principles

Trust comes from boundaries.

Disclose the AI

Callers should know when they are interacting with an automated assistant and have an appropriate alternative.

Approve before launch

Clients review scripts, FAQs, disclosures, routing, test results, and escalation rules before live traffic.

Escalate judgment

Unknown, prohibited, sensitive, or safety-related requests should not be improvised by the system.

Measure behavior

Success criteria focus on fields captured, routes followed, and exceptions found—not invented revenue attribution.

Sell supported scope

Integrations are promised only after compatibility review. Unsupported systems get a fallback or discovery phase.

Keep a human path

Transfer, callback, voicemail, or structured notification is defined for cases the system should not handle.
